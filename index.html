Data Artisans uses data obtained from Google Trends and the National Statistics Institute (INE) to create artistic 3D visualizations. The platform allows users to explore and analyze data interactively, facilitating the identification of trends and relationships that are not evident in static 2D representations.

This approach appeals to the recipient's emotions while enriching the user experience by transforming data analysis into a more intuitive and visually appealing activity.

The toroids represent the weight of housing costs.
This time, the average rental price for a 60 m2 home has been calculated for each autonomous community, using information obtained from the Idealista housing rental portal.
The green lattice structure represents the average salaries per autonomous community, sourced from the INE (National Statistics Institute).
It's worth noting that these are net average salaries per community.
The red spheres represent population density in each autonomous community, also sourced from the INE.
It's noteworthy that both Ceuta and Melilla have particularly high population densities.
The spiral with a color gradient is the most experimental statistic, as it's based on a proprietary algorithm measuring happiness levels per autonomous community.
Information was gathered from the Google Trends portal, analyzing the frequency of searches for specific words indicating mood. The spiral becomes thicker, taller, and bluer as happiness levels increase.
The brown-colored areas indicate the population count per autonomous community, contrasting with density.
This information was also obtained from the INE. As observed, Andalusia has the highest population among the autonomous communities.
The red and blue threads connect the capital of Spain with different provinces.
The colors indicate the political affiliation of Spain's two major parties: the Socialist Party and the People's Party.
