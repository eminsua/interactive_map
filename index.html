Data Artisans uses data obtained from Google Trends and the National Statistics Institute (INE) to create artistic 3D visualizations.

This approach appeals to the recipient's emotions while enriching the user experience by transforming data analysis into a more intuitive and visually appealing activity.

The toroids represent the weight of housing costs.
The green lattice structure represents the average salaries per autonomous community.
The red spheres represent population density.
The spiral represents happiness levels using Google Trends.
The brown areas indicate population count.
The red and blue threads indicate political affiliation.
